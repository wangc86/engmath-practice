⚠️ This is not an official university system

This site is run by your course instructor. It is not connected to any campus system. The account below is shared by the whole class, so treat this site as a public place: do not use a university password here, and do not type anything you would not say out loud in class.

What this site records

  • Everyone in the course signs in with the same account, so the site has no way to tell who you are
  • It keeps a count, for the class as a whole, of which topics are practised at which difficulty and which demos are opened
  • It does not store your name, your student ID, your IP address, or anything you type while working on a problem
  • There is no answer box on this site and the system does not check answers
